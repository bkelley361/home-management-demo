## Appliance Management

### Safety Recalls

- Notify users of manufacturer safety recalls.
- Alert users when an owned appliance is affected.
- Link directly to official recall information and recommended actions.

### Warranty Tracking

- Store warranty information for appliances and home systems.
- Notify users before warranties expire.
- Upload and store warranty documentation.

### Product Registration

- Register new appliances with manufacturers.
- Store registration confirmation.
- Keep purchase dates and serial numbers in one place.

### Parts Lookup

- Search for replacement parts by:
  - Manufacturer
  - Model number
  - Serial number
- Link to OEM replacement parts.
- Save commonly replaced parts.

# Future Vision

The goal is to evolve from a simple reminder app into a complete home management platform where homeowners can:

- Track every appliance and home system.
- Stay on top of maintenance schedules.
- Receive recall and warranty notifications.
- Store all home-related documents.
- Find trusted local service providers.
- Manage maintenance costs over time.
- Sync with existing productivity tools.
- Access everything from both mobile and web.
